Higher education

ISIC Rev.5 8530 – Open business blueprint for enrollment, accreditation, and degree conferral

A forkable, deployable business model for colleges and universities that need student enrollment intake, course and degree-plan assessment, grade finalization, and degree conferral—all governed by an independent Academic Integrity Governor and immutable audit ledger.

This open-source actor seals a RegistrarOps-LLM into a trust boundary that never finalizes a real grade or confers a real degree without human sign-off. Built on the same langgraph-clj StateGraph runtime as thirteen prior ISIC verticals, it extends the governed-actor pattern to the first education vertical in this fleet.

Designed for independent colleges and universities, cooperative consortia, and community-education institutions that want transparent, auditable operations and data sovereignty.
