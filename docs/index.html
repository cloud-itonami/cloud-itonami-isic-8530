Higher education

ISIC Rev.5 8530 – Open business blueprint for enrollment, accreditation, and degree conferral

The problem: LLMs excel at drafting enrollment records and checking prerequisites, but they have no notion of which jurisdiction's accreditation requirements are official, no license to finalize a real grade or confer a real degree, and no way to detect academic-integrity conflicts. Let an LLM finalize a grade directly and you risk fabricated citations, grades against unsatisfied prerequisites, degrees against insufficient credits, and unresolved academic-integrity concerns falling through.

An open-source actor that puts an independent Academic Integrity Governor in front of every grade finalization and degree conferral—the Governor independently re-verifies every high-stakes decision against jurisdiction facts, prerequisite records, and integrity flags, and rejects proposals that violate governance rules or lack proper evidence.

Forkable and deployable for accredited colleges, universities, consortia, and community-education institutions that want transparent, auditable enrollment-to-degree operations and full data sovereignty.

Not included: Full degree-audit engines with corequisites, substitution/waiver rules, transfer-credit equivalency, or major/minor/general-education distribution checking. Extend coverage by adding the next governance gate following the same independent-governor pattern.
